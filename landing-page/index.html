Automatically capture development insights and visualize them as a beautiful story.

See Timeline Demo View on GitHub

The Problem

You solve complex problems, make smart decisions, and build valuable features.
But there's no record of the thinking behind it.
Your amazing work goes unnoticed.

The Solution: 3 Commands

1

Capture

$ uroboro capture "Fixed database timeout - cut query time from 3s to 200ms"

Capture insights as you work. Takes 10 seconds.

2

Visualize

$ uroboro publish --journey

See your work as an interactive timeline at localhost:8080

3

Share

$ uroboro publish --blog

Generate shareable content from your actual work.
